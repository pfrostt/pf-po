Liberté Coffee

The most beautiful hotels in the Arctic had world class rooms, world class views, and the same coffee you would find at a gas station. I thought that was worth fixing.

01/08: the gap:

Two problems. Same root.

(B2B) / (B2C)

Some of the most intentional hospitality experiences in the world, Ice Hotel, Arctic Bath, Tree Hotel, had menus where every detail was curated except the drinks. The non-alcoholic options were water, generic juice, and whatever coffee came in the last delivery. The experience broke the moment someone ordered something that did not match the room they were sitting in.

02/08: the solution

So I built one.

Liberté is a cold brew coffee brand based in Northern Sweden, inspired by arctic nature and the core elements that connect us to it: fire and water.

The beans are wood-roasted by Antica Tostatura Triestina, a family company in Trieste, Italy that has been doing this one thing for generations. The coffee is brewed with arctic water using a cold brew method that lets the grounds release aroma at a softer, more intense level. The result is a ready-to-drink coffee that belongs in the places it is served.
