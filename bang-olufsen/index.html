Decoded Brief — Beo Grace Launch Extension

Written as a decoded version of what the Beo Grace launch brief might have said. This is the document the three executions are answering.

Project

Beo Grace launch extension. Q2 2026.

Background

Beo Grace launched in March 2026 under the line A future icon. Crafted for the century ahead. The line carries B&O’s anniversary weight but does not separate Beo Grace from its own category. Product truth supports a second creative platform the current campaign is not using. The 15% smaller form, the mirror-polished aluminium, the Italian leather pouch priced at $400, and the $1,500 entry price put Beo Grace in conversation with Cartier, not Apple.

Objective

Reframe Beo Grace from premium earbud to wearable object. Build a creative platform that can live alongside A future icon and carry the product across Instagram, web, retail and the commercial surfaces where the current execution undersells the object.

Audience

35 to 55. Owns two or more luxury accessories above $1,000. Values quiet design over visible logos. Reads Cereal, Kinfolk, Monocle. Already owns AirPods Pro and intends to keep them for the gym. Buys Beo Grace as an object, not as a device.

Proposition

Beo Grace is the first pair of earbuds engineered to be worn, not used.

Single-minded line

Kept close.

Tone

Cartier, not Apple. Patek’s restraint at Sony’s price. Declarative, not descriptive. Short sentences. No adjectives that could appear on any other premium brand. The words premium, sleek, immersive, cutting-edge, elevate, experience do not appear anywhere in the work.

Mandatories

B&O’s existing colourway names (Honey Tone, Natural Aluminium) respected. Price visible on commercial surfaces where conversion is the goal.

Deliverables
  • One Instagram post
  • One website hero section
  • One in-store poster
Success measures
  • Time on product page up 30%
  • Add-to-basket conversion on the Beo Grace category up 15%
  • Social saves per post up 50% against the H100 benchmark
The Platform

Kept close.

Two words. A quiet claim that holds two meanings at once. Beo Grace is kept physically close — it rests on the body, against the skin, at the edge of the ear. And it is kept by the few — owned, looked after, returned to, passed on.

The category competes on hearing. Beo Grace competes on intimacy. Kept close is the single piece of copy that has to be remembered, and it scales from a poster line to an Instagram caption to a hero subline without changing shape.

Supporting line — Instagram caption only

Designed in Lyngby. Polished in Struer. Kept close.

That is the long form. The short form is two words, and it is what every other surface uses.

The Brief

Current: Consumer or prosumer camera with limited dynamic range and color data.

Proposed: RED cinema camera with cinema lenses. More latitude in post, sharper image, cinematic feel. This is where the quality ceiling gets raised before anything else.

The Platform

Current: One focal length per camera. Every frame feels the same.

Proposed: Multiple lenses giving you wide, medium, and close-up options. Each serves a purpose. Wide for context, medium for conversation, close-up for emphasis.

Website hero section

Current: No leading room on the hero. Subject pressed against the frame edge. Feels cramped.

Proposed: Proper leading room, rule of thirds, intentional framing that gives the speaker space to breathe and the viewer somewhere to rest.

Instagram post

Current: Whiteboard and available room. Background is an afterthought.

Proposed: Intentional set built around the brand. Background elements, textures, and depth using the palette. The environment tells the viewer this is a brand that takes itself seriously before the speaker says a word.
