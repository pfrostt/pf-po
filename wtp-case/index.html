Cinematography

Cinematography

Current: Consumer or prosumer camera with limited dynamic range and color data.

Proposed: RED cinema camera with cinema lenses. More latitude in post, sharper image, cinematic feel. This is where the quality ceiling gets raised before anything else.

Current: One focal length per camera. Every frame feels the same.

Proposed: Multiple lenses (35mm, 50mm, 85mm) giving you wide, medium, and close-up options. Each serves a purpose. Wide for context, medium for conversation, close-up for emphasis.

Current: No leading room on B camera. Subject pressed against the frame edge. Feels cramped.

Proposed: Proper leading room, rule of thirds, intentional framing that gives the speaker space to breathe and the viewer somewhere to rest.

Current: Whiteboard and available room. Background is an afterthought.

Proposed: Intentional set built around the WTP brand. Background elements, textures, and depth using their palette. The environment tells the viewer this is a brand that takes itself seriously before the speaker says a word.

Current: Static locked-off shots on both cameras.

Proposed: Subtle movement where it serves the content. A slow push-in on a slider for emphasis. A gentle drift during key moments. Slider movements add production value and guide the viewer's eye without being distracting.

SFX and Music

The speaker carries the content. The music carries the feeling. The sound effects carry the edit. I chose a track that supports without competing and layered SFX to reinforce every transition and beat. Whooshes on cuts. Risers before key points. Soft impacts that give motion graphics weight. None of it is loud. All of it is felt.

Color Grading

Here is what I did with the footage you gave me. White balance correction, noise reduction, tonal shaping, and a grade that brings it closer to where the brand should sit visually. The result is better. But the source footage only bends so far.

With more flexible footage from a cinema camera, the same grading decisions become dramatically more effective. More detail in the shadows. More control over skin tones. A look that feels intentional instead of corrected.
